## Why this matters

`pnpm audit --prod` in `apps/web` reports **19 vulnerabilities (9 high, 8 moderate, 2 low)**, dominated by advisories against `next@15.5.9` (e.g. GHSA-3g8h-86w9-wvmq, GHSA-vfv6-92ff-j949 — both patched in `>=15.5.16`), including DoS and middleware/proxy-related issues. This app is intended to face customers (the seat renderer). A patch-line bump within 15.5.x clears the Next advisories with effectively zero migration surface.
